13 · Support, resources, decisions

What helps me deliver better systems

Timely approvals

Some automation needs paid tools or platform costs. Earlier approval or rejection avoids stalling half-finished workflows.

Clear process ownership

Knowing who owns the real business process before automating helps the workflow match actual operations.

Access & permissions

Timely access to GHL accounts, calendars, communication tools, business systems and client platforms speeds up testing and troubleshooting.

User feedback

Feedback from real users after launch lets me refine workflows against real scenarios.

Prioritization

When several requests are active, clear priorities keep development on the most important work.

Workshop resources

The remaining batches are postponed and 300+ participants are waiting. A decision on resources and timing lets me plan the restart.
